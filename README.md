# European Urology Statistical Guidelines

A one-page site collecting the four statistical reporting guidelines used by the statistical editors of the *European Urology* family of journals.

Live site: https://umdunn.github.io/EU-Statistical-Guidelines/

1. Guidelines for Reporting of Statistics for Clinical Research in Urology (2019) — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eururo.2018.12.014
2. Guidelines for Reporting of Figures and Tables for Clinical Research in Urology (2020) — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eururo.2020.04.048
3. Guidelines for Reporting Observational Research in Urology: The Importance of Clear Reference to Causality (2023) — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eururo.2023.04.027
4. Guidelines for Meta-analyses and Systematic Reviews in Urology (2025) — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eururo.2025.07.021
